### 1. Introduction
SSP Design ("we," "our," or "us") operates the SSP Theme Viewer mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

### 2. Information Collection and Use
We collect minimal information to provide, maintain, and improve our App.

### 3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any personal user data with third parties.

### 4. Children's Privacy
Our App is a portfolio showcase intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.
