1. Introduction
SSP Design ("we," "our," or "us") operates the SSP Theme Viewer mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding any data processed through our services.

2. Information Collection and Use
We collect minimal information to provide, maintain, and improve our App.

Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) Tokens: The App utilizes Google Firebase Cloud Messaging to send optional push notifications regarding new theme and watch face releases. When you launch the App, a secure device registration token is generated by Google Play Services. We do not link this token to your identity, name, email address, or personal profile.

External Links: Our App allows you to view custom phone themes and Wear OS watch faces by linking out directly to third-party stores (such as the Samsung Galaxy Store and Google Play Store). When you click these links, you are directed to external platforms governed by their own respective privacy policies.

3. Data Sharing and Disclosure
We do not sell, trade, rent, or share any personal user data with third parties.

Non-personal technical data (such as push notification tokens) is processed securely through Google Firebase infrastructure in compliance with Google's privacy guidelines.

4. Children's Privacy
Our App is a portfolio showcase intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy
